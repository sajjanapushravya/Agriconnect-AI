# 🌾 AgriConnect AI

### Better Prices. Direct Markets. Smarter Farming.

AgriConnect AI is a simple and farmer-friendly digital marketplace that connects farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) directly with consumers and bulk buyers.

The platform helps farmers sell their agricultural products, access price information, understand demand, manage orders, and plan deliveries efficiently using AI-based features.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Farmers and FPOs often face difficulties in reaching direct buyers, accessing transparent price information, predicting demand, and managing deliveries efficiently. Dependence on intermediaries can reduce direct market opportunities, while uncertain demand can make production and selling decisions difficult. Many existing digital platforms are also complex for farmers with limited digital literacy.

AgriConnect AI addresses these challenges through a simple digital marketplace that provides direct market access, price comparison, AI-based demand forecasting, farmer-friendly recommendations, order management, and route optimization.

---

## 💡 Solution

AgriConnect AI provides a single platform where:

- 🌾 Farmers can list and sell agricultural products.
- 🏢 FPOs can coordinate produce and manage bulk orders.
- 🛒 Buyers can discover and purchase agricultural products.
- 💰 Users can compare market and platform prices.
- 🤖 AI can provide demand-based recommendations.
- 📊 Demand forecasting supports better planning.
- 🚚 Route optimization helps plan deliveries efficiently.
- 📦 Orders and inventory can be managed digitally.

---

## 🤖 AI Components

### 1. AI Demand Forecasting
Uses historical sales information to estimate future demand for agricultural products and support better planning.

### 2. Agentic AI
Analyzes relevant information such as demand, price, and recent sales to generate simple recommendations for farmers.

### 3. Prompt Engineering
Uses structured prompts to convert AI insights into short and easy-to-understand recommendations.

### 4. Price Intelligence
Compares available market prices with platform prices and displays price differences and potential savings.

### 5. Route Optimization
Uses delivery locations, order quantities, and vehicle capacity to generate an efficient delivery sequence.
